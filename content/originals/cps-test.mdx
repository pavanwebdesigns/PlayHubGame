## What's measured

CPS means clicks per second. Press Start, click (or tap) as fast as you can until the timer ends, and we divide your total clicks by the seconds.

## What's a good score?

Most people land somewhere between 5 and 8 clicks per second on a mouse. Fast players often get above 10. Your score depends a lot on your mouse or screen, so compare with your own best rather than with others.

## Tips

- Keep your wrist relaxed and let your finger do the work.
- Rest your hand before each try.
- On a phone, try tapping with two fingers alternating.
